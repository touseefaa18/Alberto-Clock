Vintage

A vintage watch is generally defined as a timepiece that is at least 20 to 30 years old. Beyond mere age, true vintage pieces carry historical, cultural, or aesthetic significance tied to the watchmaking era in which they were built

Luxury

Luxury watches are high-precision timepieces valued for their exceptional craftsmanship, heritage, and mechanical engineering rather than just telling time.
