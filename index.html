— Crafted With Precision —

Technology

Every watch goes through the hands of our artisans dozens of times before it's ever finished. 200+ hours of hand-assembly, old Geneva-stripe finishing methods sitting right alongside solar movements and in-house tourbillon cages.

⚙

Vintage Movement

The vintage calibres we work with aren't just old — they're proof that some ideas were right the first time. Each one finished by hand the same way it would've been done decades ago: Geneva stripes polished stroke by stroke, perlage done with a rotating peg, bridges beveled until the light catches them just right.

✦

Luxury Craftsmanship

Real craftsmanship isn't something you advertise — it's something you notice. Our atelier has craftsmen who've been doing this for thirty years, some of them third-generation watchmakers, still particular about the smallest things. A caseback engraving. The depth of a guilloche cut.

◈

Smart Integration

Most people don't want to give up wearing a watch they love just to get notifications. We built connectivity into cases that look nothing like a smartwatch — health sensors, subtle alerts — hidden inside a classic steel or gold case. The movement is still Swiss. The dial is still hand-finished.

◉

Tourbillon Precision

A tourbillon cage takes one of our watchmakers between 60 and 80 hours to assemble — and that's just the cage. Each piece in our Prestige and Elite lines is built from scratch in-house, by one person, start to finish. Ours hold within ±1 second per day.

☀

Eco-Drive Innovation

Why should a beautiful thing need a battery? The photovoltaic cells sit underneath the dial — completely invisible — pulling energy from whatever light is around, indoor or out. No battery changes, no waste. We think luxury and responsibility belong together, and these watches are our best argument for that.

— Who We Are —

A Legacy Forged in Precision

Alberto Clocks started, honestly, with one guy and a loupe. Alberto Reyes opened the workshop in Geneva back in 1987 — no grand plan, just a deep fixation on getting mechanical movements right. He'd trained himself mostly, spent time at the Geneva school, and then quietly started restoring old Swiss calibres for people who cared enough to track him down.

Fast forward nearly four decades, and Alberto Clocks has shipped over 120,000 handmade pieces to collectors in 58 countries, picked up 14 international horology awards, and still doesn't cut corners. Every watch that leaves here is built to do what Alberto promised from day one — not just track time, but mean something.

— What Drives Us —

Our Core Values

☯

Timeless Craftsmanship

Hand-assembly is slow, expensive, and absolutely not negotiable. Our craftsmen in Geneva — most trained for over ten years — bevel bridges by hand, seat jewels by eye, and finish plates in ways that make every difference to the watch.

✶

Swiss Precision

Every movement is regulated to COSC standards, assembled in our dust-free environment, to tolerances measured in microns. Switzerland's watchmaking tradition runs through everything we do as the actual minimum we hold ourselves to.

◆

Ethical Sourcing

The gold, the stones, the leather — all sourced with documentation we can stand behind. Conflict-free, certified, farm-verified animal welfare for every hide. We're members of the Responsible Jewellery Council.

♦

Heritage Design

Every dial and case starts with a pencil drawing — no software, no shortcuts. Our designers pull from Art Deco, mid-century modernism, Victorian detailing. The shape of a watch hand is drawn by hand before any machine touches it.

— The People Behind The Dial —

Meet Our Founders

AR

Alberto Reyes

Founder & Master Horologist

Alberto Reyes taught himself the basics before anyone took him seriously, then pushed his way into the Ecole d'Horlogerie de Genève and didn't leave until he'd mastered the tourbillon. His tourbillon designs have shown at Baselworld twice and picked up two Grand Prix d'Horlogerie de Genève nominations.

in
MR

Margot Reyes

Co-Founder & Creative Director

Margot came from the Royal College of Art in London with strong opinions about color and a willingness to defend them in a room full of engineers. She's the reason our dials look the way they do — Vogue, Robb Report, Wallpaper* have all taken notice.

in
LV

Luca Verdi

Head of Movement Engineering

Before Alberto Clocks, Luca spent years at a Swiss manufacture in Le Locle filing for patents — 22 of them, mostly around escapement geometry and micro-rotor design. He now leads 18 engineers who share that same stubbornness about doing it right.

in
SP

Sofia Petit

Director of Heritage & Restoration

Sofia Petit brings over 16 years of hands-on experience restoring antique timepieces from across Europe. Trained under master restorers in Lyon and later at the Patek Philippe Museum, she oversees our bespoke restoration programme.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I request watch repair or restoration?

Contact our concierge team using the consultation form. Include your watch reference, a description of the issue and any relevant details. Select Heritage Restoration & Service as your consultation type.

How do I arrange routine watch servicing?

Contact Alberto to enquire about a servicing assessment. Our team can review your timepiece information and advise you on the next steps.

How can I check my warranty?

Refer to your purchase documentation for the warranty terms applicable to your watch. You can also contact our concierge team with your model reference for assistance.

How can I enquire about a watch?

Visit our New Arrivals section to explore the catalogue. Each watch has a Quick View option displaying its price, collection and specifications. For additional enquiries, contact our team.

Can I request a bespoke timepiece?

Yes, you can submit a bespoke commission enquiry through the consultation form. Select Bespoke Timepiece Commission and describe your requirements.
